Aeron

Ergonomic to the core, Aeron was designed for all types of human bodies. 
For decades, we’ve been perfecting it so that it’s not only good for your body, 
but good for our environment too. Its launch was revolutionary in the office 
furniture industry—and it continues to lead the charge, thanks to updated 
research, material innovation, and an iconic design that lasts. It’s the same 
ergonomic comfort you’d expect from Aeron, updated with two new colors 
that expand the palette, including a midnight blue (Nightfall) and a dark olive 
green (Jasper).

Aeron® Chair
Statement of 
line overview
Details
1.	 8Z Pellicle® 
8Z Pellicle offers unprecedented comfort 
through eight varied zones of tension in 
the seat and back.
2.	 PostureFit SL® 
Stabilizes and supports lower back to 
nurture natural S-shape of the spine 
and activate healthful posture.
3.	 Harmonic™ 2 Tilt 
Helps the chair move with you thanks 
to an innovative tilt mechanism under 
the seat.
4.	 Three sizes 
Aeron comes in three sizes to ensure a 
more inclusive fit.
5.	 Dematerialized base 
The aluminum base option has been 
redesigned, reducing overall material 
use and carbon footprint.
6.	 Ocean-bound plastic 
Aeron is composed of more than 
50% recycled material, including 
ocean-bound plastic.
