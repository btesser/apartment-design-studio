HIS OFFICE — TWO COHESIVE DARK DESIGN ALTERNATIVES

Start with His-office-B-C-design-review.pdf for the shared plan, real products,
budgets and three matched comparisons of B and C.

Open his-office-viewer.html in a current desktop browser to explore the actual
3D room and switch between B Charcoal + Slat Bay and C Naval Studio. The viewer
is self-contained; it does not need a server or an internet connection. The
included his-office-viewer-source.zip contains editable source and instructions.

The six final appearance studies are the original generator PNGs:
  variants/b-charcoal-slat/images/concept-a.png, concept-b.png, concept-c.png
  variants/c-ink-studio/images/concept-a.png, concept-b.png, concept-c.png

Each variant's model/ folder contains his-office-design.blend, portable GLB
files, its exact furniture layout, camera poses and the corresponding source
renders. The models define proposed dimensions and placement; generated photos
illustrate finishes and atmosphere.

layout/final-furnished-plan.pdf is the full dimensioned standalone plan.
DESIGN-BRIEF.txt explains the composition and practical limits. Product URLs,
prices and original photographs are in products/selected-products.json.
Source hashes and final fit/image checks accompany the package. FILE-MANIFEST.json
lists the packaged files and their SHA-256 hashes.
