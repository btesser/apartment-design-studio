HIS OFFICE — FINAL B/C MODEL AUTHORITY

The current furnished models are under variants/b-charcoal-slat/model/ and
variants/c-ink-studio/model/. Their his-office-design.blend files, layout.json,
GLB exports, camera-poses.json, final-artifacts.json and source renders define
the proposed dimensions, furniture placement and facing. Root-level model files
are preparation/checkpoint evidence, including the earlier UPLIFT and Branch
chair stages; they are not the final furniture authority.

The final proposals use the selected Tria desk, ONE owned Mineral Aeron Size C,
clear project bench, TWO MOSSLANDA ledges, grouped Richmond art, lit clothes
console and small dresser palm. B has a five-panel Black Ash bay and Peppercorn
two-wall wrap; C has smooth Naval on the same two walls.

Three native camera angles per alternative were built and checked. Initial
imagegen calls used three model angles and two source boards, but repeated a
camera. Final B-angle calls used only the exact target projection plus the two
boards. Final A/C edits used a prior generator original, exact target projection
and the two boards. Generation attempts, exact prompts, ordered references and
source/output hashes are preserved under images/. Generated concepts illustrate
appearance; the native model controls measured geometry. Final PNGs remain
untouched generator originals.

The final nominal 60 cm person-circle route passes with the Aeron working or
pulled back 45 cm; 61 cm fails when pulled. Open drawers and full chair pullback
are sequential operations. See layout/final-furnished-plan.pdf and the sealed
reports under qa/final-variants/. Scan uncertainty is about 5–12 cm; model fit
is planning evidence, not a surveyed clearance or installation guarantee.
