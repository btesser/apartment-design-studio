Details

Tap into your flow with the all-in-one Jarvis Standing Desk. Designed to promote wellness and movement, Jarvis encourages a variety of sitting and standing positions throughout the workday with a customizable setup. Easily adjust the height of the bamboo tabletop with the push of a button on an up-down touch pad or an OLED programmable handset. With the option of a powered grommet, you can easily charge devices right on your desktop. This is the authentic Jarvis Standing Desk by the Oregon-based company Fully. Fully is a Certified B Corporation dedicated to enhancing the quality of your work and workspace with high-quality design. With an eye toward making a positive impact on customers, as well as the planet, Fully’s products are responsibly produced and packaged. Jarvis’s tabletop is made of bamboo, chosen both for its natural beauty and rapid renewability. The bamboo, which releases 35% more oxygen than an equivalent stand of trees, is grown without pesticides or fertilizers. When the plant is fully mature in just five years, it’s harvested by hand to preserve the root structure. Plus, Jarvis is shipped in 100% recyclable and EPE-free packaging. If selecting the grommet option, the white Jarvis Desk will ship with black grommets installed, and white grommets will ship separately.
  • OLED programmable handset: press one of four height presets to adjust; height is indicated on screen.
  • Up-down toggle handset: press down to lower, lift up to raise.
  • Power grommet option available.
  • Lift speed of 1.5 inches per second, capacity of 350 lbs.
  • Noise level less than 50 dB.
  • Backed by a 1-year warranty on power grommets and surge protectors; 5-year warranty on tops; 7-year warranty for all desk frame components, mechanical parts, motors and electrical components.
