FAQs:

What makes a rug good for office chairs?

A great rug for office chair use needs a low pile so wheels roll smoothly without snagging. Ours also have a slip-resistant backing to stay in place, even with constant movement, making them ideal for rolling chairs and active work setups.

What size rug is best for a home office setup?

It depends on your layout. For small desks or bedroom setups, a 3'x5' or 5'x7' rug works well. For larger offices with more furniture, a 6'x9' rug helps anchor the entire workspace. Just make sure the rug extends beyond your desk and chair to allow free movement.

Are these office rugs washable?

Absolutely. Every rug in this collection is machine-washable, so coffee spills, snack crumbs, or everyday dust can be quickly cleaned without fuss.

Will Ruggable office rugs stay flat under a rolling chair?

Yes. Our rugs are designed to lay flat and stay in place, even with frequent chair movement. You won’t need to constantly readjust or worry about bunching in the middle of a call.

What styles of office rugs do you offer?

You’ll find a wide range—from clean-lined modern styles to vintage-inspired prints, soft neutrals, bold graphics, and minimalist tones. Whether you're going full executive or softening a corner workspace, there's a rug that fits your look.
