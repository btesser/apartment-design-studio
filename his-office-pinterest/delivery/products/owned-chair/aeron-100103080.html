Details

The Aeron Chair revolutionized office seating with its defining design qualities: the pioneering 8Z Pellicle suspension material and its patented PostureFit SL back support, which affords the ideal sitting position—chest open, shoulders back, pelvis tilted slightly forward. Combining a deep knowledge of human-centered design with cutting-edge technology, the Aeron Chair is engineered with state-of-the-art adjustability, including the arms, seat height, and recline, to help you maintain the optimal posture so you can focus on the task at hand. It’s offered in three sizes, bringing the greatest comfort to the widest range of users.
  • Breathable suspension keeps you cool and comfortable.
  • Three chair sizes deliver comfort to nearly every body type.
  • Adjustable PostureFit SL pads provide lumbar support and stabilize the base of the spine.
  • Harmonic Tilt supports the way you naturally move, for a smooth and balanced recline.
  • Fully adjustable arms (height, depth, and angle) allow for a custom fit.
  • Composed of recycled materials, Aeron's production helps divert plastic waste from the ocean.
Aeron is up to 91% recyclable based on available recycling facilities. Aeron is composed of more than 50% recycled material, including ocean-bound plastic. Aeron is BIFMA Level 3 certified and has also received the Indoor Advantage Gold certification, SCS Global Services' highest level of indoor air quality performance for furniture. The certification ensures that furniture products support a healthy indoor environment by meeting strict chemical emission limits for volatile organic compounds (VOCs).

Frequently Asked Questions

The Aeron Chair's construction contributes to its longevity. Die-cast aluminum and polymer components resist wear better than wooden or plastic parts. The 8Z Pellicle suspension material maintains its tension and support properties longer than foam, which compresses and deteriorates over time. The mechanical components—tilt mechanism, pneumatic cylinder, and adjustment controls—use industrial-grade materials designed for repeated daily use.

Herman Miller backs the Aeron Chair with a 12-year warranty covering manufacturing defects and component failures. This warranty period indicates confidence in the chair's durability, as many Aeron Chairs remain functional for decades with proper care.

The Aeron Chair distinguishes itself through three core innovations that work together to create superior sitting support.

8Z Pellicle Suspension Material
The chair uses an elastomeric suspension mesh instead of traditional foam padding. This breathable material distributes weight evenly across eight zones of varying tension. The suspension keeps air circulating, preventing heat buildup during long work sessions. Unlike foam that compresses over time, the Pellicle material maintains consistent support throughout the chair's lifespan.

PostureFit SL Back Support
Herman Miller's patented PostureFit SL system targets the sacral and lumbar regions of the spine. Two independent pads flex separately: the lower pad stabilizes the sacrum while the upper pad supports the lumbar curve. This dual-pad design positions the pelvis slightly forward, which naturally opens the chest and pulls shoulders back. Users can adjust both pads to match their specific spinal curvature.

Harmonic Tilt Mechanism
The Harmonic 2 Tilt system allows natural reclining movement. The mechanism balances the chair automatically as users shift weight, creating smooth transitions between upright and reclined positions. A tension control knob lets users calibrate the effort required to recline based on body weight and preference.

The Aeron Chair addresses back pain through targeted spinal support rather than generalized cushioning. The chair maintains the spine's natural S-curve by stabilizing the sacrum, the triangular bone at the base of the spine where many back issues originate. When the sacrum tilts properly forward, the entire spine aligns correctly. This positioning reduces strain on spinal discs and surrounding muscles.

The Aeron Chair is available in three sizes to accommodate different body dimensions, so the easiest way to tell if your chair is an A, B, or C is to match its specifications to one of the following descriptions:

  • Size A (Small): Recommended for users 4'10" to 5'4" tall and up to 300 pounds. The seat height adjusts from 14.75 to 19 inches. Overall dimensions measure 38.5 inches high, 25.75 inches wide, and 16 inches deep. The chair weighs 40 pounds.

  • Size B (Medium): Fits users 5'2" to 6' tall and up to 350 pounds. Seat height ranges from 16 to 20.5 inches. Dimensions are 41 inches high, 27 inches wide, and 16.75 inches deep. This size weighs 41 pounds and is the most commonly selected option.

  • Size C (Large): Designed for users 5'10" to 6'6" tall and up to 400 pounds. Seat height adjusts between 16 and 20.5 inches. The chair measures 43 inches high, 28.25 inches wide, and 18.5 inches deep, weighing 43 pounds.

Weight capacity varies by chair size. Size A supports up to 300 pounds. Size B accommodates up to 350 pounds. Size C handles up to 400 pounds.

The Aeron Chair does not include a headrest as part of its standard design or available factory options. The chair's engineering focuses on supporting the spine from the sacrum through the lumbar region, not the cervical spine or head. This design philosophy stems from research showing that proper lumbar and sacral support naturally encourages correct head and neck positioning.

After-market headrest attachments exist, but Herman Miller does not manufacture or endorse them. These third-party accessories may affect the chair's warranty and performance characteristics.

Herman Miller provides specific sizing recommendations based on height and weight. Size A suits users 4'10"–5'4" tall and up to 300 pounds. Size B accommodates users 5'2"–6' tall and up to 350 pounds. Size C fits users 5'10"–6'6" tall and up to 400 pounds.

Herman Miller remastered the Aeron Chair in 2016, more than two decades after the original 1994 introduction. The remaster maintained the chair's iconic silhouette while updating materials, finishes, and ergonomic features.

The remastered Aeron introduced several improvements over the classic version. The updated 8Z Pellicle material offers more sophisticated zonal support with eight distinct tension zones compared to the original's simpler suspension. Herman Miller also refined the PostureFit SL system to provide more precise sacral and lumbar adjustment.

Material changes include new frame finishes like Onyx Ultra Matte and updated base options. The remastered version offers Mineral, Nightfall, and Jasper frame colors alongside the classic Graphite and Black. Polished aluminum bases joined the existing finish options.

The tilt mechanism received updates for smoother operation. The remaster added Quiet Roll technology to the multi-surface casters, reducing noise on hard floors and providing subtle braking when the chair is unoccupied.

There are many ways to adjust your Aeron Chair. The pneumatic seat height lever sits beneath the right side of the seat. Pull the lever upward while seated to lower the chair. Pull the lever while standing or lifting your weight off the seat to raise it.

The tilt tension knob, located under the front center of the seat, controls recline resistance. Turn the knob clockwise to increase tension, making recline more difficult. Turn counterclockwise to decrease tension for easier recline. Adjust until you can recline smoothly without the chair pushing you forward or falling back too easily.

For chairs equipped with the Tilt Limiter and Seat Angle feature, a second control lets you set maximum recline range. This adjustment offers three positions: upright, mid-recline, and full-recline. The seat angle adjustment tilts the seat forward up to 5 degrees for users who prefer working in a slightly forward position.

PostureFit SL adjustment uses two dials on the back of the chair. The upper dial controls lumbar support depth and height. The lower dial adjusts sacral support. Turn each dial until you feel comfortable support in both regions.

Arm adjustments vary by model. Height-adjustable arms feature a button on the underside of each arm. Press and hold the button while raising or lowering the arm. Fully adjustable arms include additional controls for depth and angle adjustment.

The Aeron Chair provides comprehensive ergonomic support through multiple adjustable features. The patented PostureFit SL back support system uses two independent pads that flex individually. The chair's Harmonic Tilt mechanism supports natural body movement during recline. The system provides smooth, balanced motion that maintains proper posture throughout the recline range.

Additional ergonomic features include:

  • Height-adjustable arms that move from 6.8 inches to 10.8 inches above the seat

  • Fully adjustable arms with forward-backward range of 2.5 inches and pivoting capability

  • Pneumatic seat height adjustment accommodating different desk heights and user preferences

  • Three size options (A, B, C) fitting users from 4'10" to 6'6" tall

The 8Z Pellicle elastomeric suspension material forms the seat and backrest. This breathable mesh distributes body weight evenly while allowing air circulation.

Aeron Chairs feature a frame and base color system with multiple finish options. Current frame and base combinations include:


  • Black with Onyx Ultra Matte finish

  • Black with Polished Aluminum

  • Graphite with Graphite finish

  • Graphite with Polished Aluminum

  • Mineral with Satin Aluminum

  • Mineral with Polished Aluminum

  • Nightfall with Nightfall finish

  • Nightfall with Polished Aluminum

  • Jasper with Jasper finish

  • Jasper with Polished Aluminum

Herman Miller provides a 12-year warranty on the Aeron Chair. This warranty covers materials and workmanship defects under normal use conditions.

Don Chadwick and Bill Stumpf collaborated to create the Aeron Chair for Herman Miller. Their research focused on how the body moves and changes position throughout the workday. This human-centered approach informed every aspect of the chair's development, from the suspension material to the adjustable components.
