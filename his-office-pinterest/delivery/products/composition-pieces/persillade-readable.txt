What is earthenware?
Ceramic is heat-resistant and durable – and the base material is types of clay. It has been used by humans to make practical objects for thousands of years. Earthenware is a ceramic type made of clay that gives an opaque surface. At IKEA we use earthenware primarily for home decor products like plant pots.
