    Archontophoenix cunninghamiana "King Palm"

    Archontophoenix cunninghamiana "King Palm" Giant Fishtail Palm. We were the first to introduce this amazing palm from Thailand in 1986. Our current inventory was personally collected by Jungle Jack's in 1995 from a remote location on the Thai Laos border at an altitude of approximately 6000ft/2000m. Fishtails from this collection are more wind-resistant than those from lower elevations elsewhere in Thailand. Please note: in young fishtail plants, it is normal to have brown leaf edges and spots

    Chamaedorea plumosa ‘Baby Queen’

    Baby Queen (TM) ~4 plants per pot, 6-7’. One gallon size is shippable. By combining the plumose foliage of the all-too-common Queen Palm with a trunk reminiscent of bamboo, Baby Queen palms create a neat package that won't exceed 15' in height. It's a great accent for any yard, and is the perfect complement for today's world of smaller yards. It does extremely well in both interior (where it's a perfect substitute for messy bamboo) and exteriors, making it an incredibly versatile plant.

    Howea forsteriana 'Kentia Palm'

    Kentia Palm, Thatch Palm (sun grown). The Jungle Jack Kentia is one of the finest Kentias available. It's a signature, coastal California grown plant, with roots developed in a custom soil mix that makes our Kentias more durable than any Hawaiian-grown plant and most other California palms. For Interiorscapes: one gallon size is shippable. 3-4 plants per pot. 8" pot ~3-4’ tall, 10" pot ~4-5’ tall. Note: Larger sizes are for pickup in Vista.
