2. Restrained Japandi — functional fit 8.4/10
Clean and calm; natural wicker cat tree integrates well. Pale all-wood treatment competes with dark Pheasantwood unless the contrast is deliberate. Full slat walls and many shelves add expense, obstructions and bulk.
Borrow quiet rug, empty worktop and closed drawers; omit slat rebuild, large plants and decorative chair as the main task chair.

3. Soft industrial monochrome — functional fit 7.6/10
Strong adult contrast and good compatibility with existing brick/industrial desk. Risks making this narrow 14m² room heavy and visually technical. White lamp remains more conspicuous than in warm studio.
Borrow black hardware and cable discipline; avoid painting every wall charcoal or adding a full dark cabinet wall.

Selected direction: Warm architectural studio. Favor space to work, calm visual rhythm, dark warm wood, existing warm white and brick, charcoal detailing and quiet stone textiles. Retain real keeper colors.
