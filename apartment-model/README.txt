This model is built from the original Polycam mesh and Amy Wu's source plan, with the scan's metric coordinates preserved. It is a design review model, not a construction survey. No geometry or furniture was derived from a generated image.

Open integrated.blend in Blender, or integrated-apartment.glb in a 3D viewer. The default design includes the source-sized dresser and provisional entry staging. The dresser leaves approximately 0.47 m at the bed foot: drawer use and circulation need a layout decision. Turn its named separate collection off to inspect the alternative. Furniture envelopes with no verified product dimensions remain explicit assumptions in the canonical furniture manifest.

Native coordinates are meters, Blender Z-up. GLTF coordinate mapping is(x,y,z)=(BlenderX,BlenderZ,-BlenderY). There is no stretching, normalization, or mirroring. Walls are generalized from recorded surfaces with roughly 0.05–0.12 m uncertainty. Floor and ceiling plane modes vary by room and by several centimeters within rooms. Listing dimensions are approximate and were used as crosschecks rather than forcing the scan to match labels.

Visible design geometry is an independently modeled shell using measured room outlines, registered openings, source-sized furnishings, approximate fixed-fixture forms, and source-supported fireplaces. The retained scan and its original photographs are separately toggleable. Floor/ceiling/wall backing repairs are separate from the clean shell. Original 8_21_2026.glb and scan.blend were never overwritten.

Interior floor occlusion gaps were filled with continuous architectural planes. Twelve stair treads match scan progression; the upper two treads/landing are inferred continuation. Verified doors and windows were retained as openings. A confidently identifiable texture-black captured-person artifact was removed only in a scan copy, using a narrow coordinate volume and dark-face filter; see repair-log.json.

Lower bathroom, laundry, and boiler/utility interiors were not recorded. Their additional blue layer is inferred from the listing plan and is separately toggleable, hidden in default design photos. No exact dimensions or unseen door hardware are certified. The Her-office low hearth includes a shallow hull infill over partial actual scan support; its missing portions remain inferred.
