About this item
LUXURY MEETS MODERN DESIGN: This 59-inch modern cat tree features natural pear wood branches, 3 handwoven beds, a cozy hammock, and a bottom den, all wrapped in soft brown cloud fleece fabric. Your cat will feel like walking on clouds while the design complements your furniture and adds a luxurious touch to your home.
FOR LARGE CATS WEIGHING UP TO 25 POUNDS: This wood cat tower is designed to accommodate 4–5 large cats, each weighing up to 25 pounds, with a total weight capacity of up to 150 pounds. Built with durable materials, the rattan cat tree offers a safe and stylish space for your cats to lounge, climb, and play, ensuring long-lasting stability and comfort.
TRIPLE BASE – 100% STABILITY & SAFETY: Built for stability, this solid wood cat tree for large cats features a triple-thickened base, solid 3.5-inch posts, and an oversized 22 × 23.6-inch base. The reinforced structure and high-quality drilled connections eliminate wobbling, ensuring a safe space for climbing, playing, and relaxing.
PREMIUM MATERIALS – DURABILITY & COMFORT: Crafted from natural solid wood, this aesthetic cat tree features a sturdy pear wood trunk wrapped in sisal rope for lasting strength. The ultra-soft 500 g/m² plush fabric provides a cozy and comfortable experience. The real wood cat tree ensures durability and a secure space for your cat to enjoy.
DESIGNED FOR HAIRLESS CATS AND MAINE COONS: This 5-tier rattan cat tree tower is specially designed for hairless cats, Maine Coons, and multi-cat households. Each level is covered in premium cloud fleece fabric to keep your cats warm, and the multi-level structure offers plenty of space for climbing, lounging, and exploring, providing endless entertainment for your cats.
