HIS OFFICE — WARM MODERN STUDIO

Open His-office-design-review.pdf for the complete proposal, measured plan,
real product photographs, prices, links and three model/concept comparisons.

Open his-office-viewer.html in a desktop browser for the offline 3D walkthrough.
Use Orbit to inspect the whole room or Walk to look around at eye level.
The viewpoint menu opens the same room angles used for image generation.
The model and its textures are embedded; an internet connection is not needed.

The design preserves the owned 42 x 30 inch Pheasantwood UPLIFT desk,
white Honeywell 02E Pro floor lamp and MUTTROS cat tree. It adds a separate
140 x 60 cm desk with office drawers, two task chairs, folded-clothes drawers,
provisional shoe racks in the existing closet, a teal visitor chair, a 6 x 9 ft
graphic rug and two blue art prints. Existing hanging storage stays in the closet.

The Blender file is editable and contains packed textures. The GLB is a
portable combined room model; separate shell, door and furniture GLBs are
also supplied. All models retain the original Polycam scale in metres.
Blender uses Z-up; GLB uses Y-up. The canonical furniture positions and
orientations are in model/layout.json.

The room outline is approximately 4.90 x 3.10 m with a closet notch,
about 14.03 square metres of usable floor and a 3.02 m ceiling. Typical
scan-outline uncertainty is 5–12 cm. This is a design model, not a survey.
The owned desk size is confirmed by the order screenshot. Retail product
dimensions are nominal, and some product forms are photograph-based models.

The checked arrangement retains a nominal 50 cm planning-person route from
entry to rear door, including simultaneous 45 cm chair pullback. The more
conservative 55 cm route does not pass. Tuck the main task chair toward its
desk before using the dresser comfortably. Check real door swings, closet
internals, cat-tree branch reach and lamp-base spacing before ordering or
installation. Recorded doors are closed; hiding them is only a viewing aid.
The STORKLINTA drawers require its Anchor/Unlock wall fixing. Confirm the
approved fixing or spacer for the brick wall, or move the chest closer to
the wall during installation; the rendered rear gap does not prove kit fit.

The three generated images in images/ illustrate the materials and overall
look. They were generated from multiple angles of this 3D model plus original
online product photographs. For dimensions, furniture placement and facing,
use the model and dimensioned plan. The images are not measurement evidence.
The separate images/attempts folder contains earlier images used as inputs
to targeted corrections; use concept-a.png, concept-b.png and concept-c.png
as the accepted final concepts. Reference sheets and hashes are included.

Product links, variants and prices are in products/selected-products.json.
Proposed new pieces including both task chairs and art frames total about
$2,344.43 before tax and shipping at the research date, October 8, 2026.
Stock and promotional prices can change. No purchases were made.

Provenance and fit checks are included in sources/ and qa/. The original
scan and earlier apartment deliverables have been left separate.
