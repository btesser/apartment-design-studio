His Office room geometry and reference pack

This pack uses the original Polycam recording, calibrated orthographic views and measured scan surfaces. Amy's furniture placements are excluded from the fresh design. Original metre scale is preserved.

The room's generalized floor envelope is 4.90m along X × 3.10m along Y. The main rectangle is 4.20 × 3.10m, with a .70m brick-side extension toward the living entrance. Generalized usable floor area is 14.028m², excluding the unrecorded hanging-closet interior. Floor Z≈1.575m; ceiling Z≈4.595m; local height≈3.02m. The listing's approximate rear-bedroom dimensions are 4.724 × 2.997m, within 3–4% of the scan's overall envelope. Neither source is a survey.

Axes: +X toward the front/living room; -X toward the rear/backyard. +Y is the white wall/kitchen side; -Y is the exposed-brick/fireplace side. +Z is up. Blender [X,Y,Z] maps to glTF [X,Z,-Y]. Generalized outlines have about 5–12cm uncertainty; calibrated openings about 3–6cm, with greater uncertainty where the scan has holes.

The most consequential fixed-feature corrections are the actual closet door and overhead cupboard; the wider ~1.08m living-door frame; the white six-panel rear door; the smaller, higher rear sash window than the prior proxy; the radiator beside the rear door at the brick corner; and the rear white pipe/front shelf nook. The brick fireplace has a full-height chimney face at Y=-2.722m and recessed white arched infill at Y=-2.835m. Its closed appearance does not establish operability. A forward rectangular white mantel/trim in the old proxy is not observed in the recording.
