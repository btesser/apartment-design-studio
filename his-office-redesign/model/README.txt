ROOM AND FIXED FEATURES
All native units are meters, Blender Z-up. GLTF maps (X,Z,-Y), Y-up. There is no
recentering, mirror, room stretching or global scale adjustment. The generalized
irregular outline spans about 4.90 x 3.10 m, area 14.03 m2, with a closet notch.
Floor Z=1.575 m, ceiling Z=4.595 m, modeled height 3.02 m. The scan-derived outline
and reconstructed wall/opening envelopes remain approximate, not a survey.

The single exterior-wall window is registered to the raw scan at Y[-0.96,-0.14],
Z[2.53,4.37]. Its lower decorative apron is separate. The adjacent exterior door
and living entry retain their verified frames; the living frame is about 1.08 m
wide. The full-height closet door and overhead cupboard, cast-iron radiator in
the brick-side rear corner, pipe and shallow entry shelves are preserved as
conservative fixed-feature proxies. Small profiles and hardware are approximate.

The full-height brick chimney projects to Y=-2.722, with adjacent brick face at
Y=-2.837. The closed white arch is recessed to approximately Y=-2.835. There is
no invented white rectangular mantel. A 4.7 cm floor continuation strip reaches
the corrected brick face while the canonical nominal floor polygon stays intact.
The uncertain broad hearth keepout is clearance metadata, not an invented solid.

Both exterior and living doors were recorded closed. Door-leaf visibility is a
separate layer; an open viewing state does not prove hinge or swing direction.
The closet interior was not recorded. Shoe racks and a small interior support
are a conditional fit idea behind the closed leaf, not measured closet geometry.

PRODUCT AND FIT BASIS
The owned UPLIFT top is the confirmed 42 x 30 inch Pheasantwood solid-wood order,
with barkline front, square back, two nickel grommets and industrial C-frame.
A seated 0.74 m operating height is an assumption. The Honeywell white 02E Pro
and MUTTROS 59 inch brown tree are kept. Cat base and height are product-sized;
its three basket/branch positions and upper reach are provisional because the
manufacturer does not publish the complete upper envelope.

The second real work surface is a 140 x 60 cm LAGKAPTEN top with one left ALEX
pedestal and two right ADILS legs. Official ALEX and STORKLINTA CAD geometry is
used with sub-centimeter normalization to chosen nominal dimensions. Two Branch
Ergonomic Pro chairs are black, face their respective work surfaces and retain
manufacturer mesh detail. The EKENASET visitor chair is a dimensioned form proxy.
The 6 x 9 Inkdrop rug and two Desenio art pieces use exact selected source photos;
UV mapping selects the textile body without editing source pixels. Thin black
art frames are an appearance assumption. Three GREJIG racks stack inside the
unrecorded closet; verify its actual interior before purchase or installation.

Independent route planning passes a 50 cm diameter circle in the working layout
and with both task chairs pulled back 45 cm. A 55 cm route fails. This is a tight
planning result, not an accessibility certification. Cat branches, lamp feet,
actual chair positions, door swings, closet interior and radiator/pipe setbacks
need on-site confirmation. Product proxies do not verify installation tolerances.

The generated finish concepts use these model views as references. The saved
model controls dimensions and furniture directions; image generation is not a
metric verification. Scripts and source manifests are included for reproducibility.
