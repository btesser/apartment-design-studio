APARTMENT DESIGN MODEL — REVISED GEOMETRY REVIEW

Open apartment-walkthrough.html in a recent desktop browser with WebGL.
The standalone viewer embeds its assets and works offline. Choose a room,
then use Orbit to inspect it or Walk to move through it. W/A/S/D move;
Escape releases the mouse. Save view exports a PNG.

This model replaces the earlier image-generated room samples for geometry
and furniture placement. All new images use the same fixed 3D scene.

MODEL LAYERS
Measured shell: a simplified architectural reconstruction in the original
Polycam metre coordinate frame, with doors/windows retained.
Furniture: dimensioned visual proxies from Amy Wu's proposal and shopping
list. Shapes and decorative details are approximate.
Recorded scan: the original capture, including recording artifacts.
Filled gaps: inferred surface continuations; these can be highlighted.

EVIDENCE AND LIMITS
The original Polycam capture was not rescaled to match the schematic.
Amy's plan was registered by doors, closets, windows and fireplaces.
Listing dimensions are approximate. Her office's short dimension differs
by about 13% between listing and scan; the model preserves the scan.
Generalized wall outlines have an estimated 0.05–0.12 m uncertainty.
Existing upstairs sofa and standing-desk dimensions are estimates.
The king bed retailer exposes inconsistent variant fields; a conservative
king-size envelope is used and marked for confirmation.
The proposed curtain divider is movable, not an existing measured wall.
Its rail height is modeled conservatively below the low beam; the selected
full curtain length needs field fitting. Entry furnishing locations are
provisional because Amy supplied products but no positions for that area.
The nearly 3 m dresser leaves about 0.47 m at the foot of the bed in the
modeled proposal. Toggle it off to compare circulation. It was not shrunk
to make the layout look more spacious.
Unrecorded lower utility interiors are identified as inferred or missing.
Surface repairs are visual reconstructions, not a survey or construction
specification. Verify tight fits and opening sizes in the apartment.
